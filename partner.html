Partner with DATAsaster

DATAsaster is a student-built demonstration for Philippine disaster information. Government agencies and media outlets can take part with their own accounts. Taking part does not mean an agency or outlet endorses the project, and DATAsaster is not an official warning channel.

Government agencies and disaster offices

  • Assess community reports of the hazard types the agency covers: corroborated, not corroborated, duplicate, under review or unable to verify.
  • Verify and resolve reports of those hazard types.
  • Publish, revise and withdraw official advisories, always with a link to the original source. Every revision is kept.
  • Cannot delete community reports, act on other hazard types, or change another agency's records.

Media outlets

  • Add incident reports that are shown with the outlet name, so readers can tell a newsroom report from an anonymous one.
  • Cannot resolve, verify or delete anything, including their own reports.
  • Media reports are still unverified. The public can flag any report as fake or wrong, and the DATAsaster team reviews flags.

How it works

  1. Write to us with the name of your agency or outlet and an authorized representative.
  2. We confirm who the person is and that they may act for the organization.
  3. We create an individual account. Accounts are not shared, and a person who leaves must be reported so access can be removed.
  4. A short test period uses demonstration data.
  5. The account goes live. Staff sign in at Settings, then Account.

What to expect

  • Actions by agency accounts are logged. Access can be suspended or removed at any time.
  • There is no 24/7 monitoring, no guaranteed uptime and no emergency response. Do not rely on DATAsaster to reach the public in an emergency.
  • There is no advertising and no paid placement.
