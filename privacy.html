Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

DATAsaster is a student-built demonstration web app for Philippine disaster information. This page explains what information the app handles and why. Contact: [your email address].

1. What you share when you post a report

  • The hazard type, severity, headline, details, place name and the other fields you fill in.
  • The location of the report: a spot you pick on the map, or your device location if you tap "Use my location".
  • Up to 3 photos. The app shrinks them before sending.
  • The time of the report.

Reports are public. Anyone who opens the app can see the text, photos and location of a shared report. Do not include faces, number plates, identity documents, phone numbers or home addresses of private people. Do not post anything you do not want others to see.

2. Your anonymous ID

When you open the app it signs you in silently with a random anonymous ID (Firebase Authentication). We do not ask for your name or email. The ID is stored with your reports so that you, and only you on that device, can mark them resolved or delete them. If you clear your browser data you get a new ID and can no longer manage your old reports.

3. Flags

If you flag a report, we store the report ID, your anonymous ID, an optional reason of up to 200 characters, and the time.

4. Agency and administrator accounts

People who help run or moderate the app, or who act for an agency, sign in with an email address and password. Their actions (verifying, assessing, resolving, publishing or withdrawing advisories, deleting) are recorded in an activity log with their account ID, their agency name and the time. Agency assessments and advisories are public.

5. Stored on your device

The app saves settings, filters, alert preferences, reports you wrote on this device, the time of your last post, and a cached copy of the app so it can work offline. This uses your browser's local storage, IndexedDB and cache. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies. Some services listed below, such as Google reCAPTCHA, may use cookies or similar technology themselves. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

6. Location

Your browser asks permission before the app reads your location. It is used to fill in a report location, sort by distance and show nearby alerts. Distance sorting and alerts are worked out on your device. Your location is only stored if you post a report with it.

7. Other services that receive data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check): stores reports, photos, flags, advisories, assessments and the activity log, and signs you in.
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise: checks that requests come from a real visitor (spam protection).
  • Vercel: hosts the site and collects aggregated page-view statistics.
  • Map and data providers: map tiles (Esri, CARTO, OpenStreetMap contributors), RainViewer, Open-Meteo, NASA EONET, USGS and EMSC. Your device contacts them directly, so they can see your IP address and the area you are viewing.
  • News: headlines come from Google News feeds through the rss2json and allorigins services.
  • Google Maps: the "Street view" button opens Google Maps in a new tab under Google's own policy.

8. How long we keep information

Reports, assessments, advisories and their revisions stay until the author or an administrator removes them. Advisory revisions and the activity log are kept for accountability. Flags are kept until reviewed.

9. Your choices and rights

You can delete your own reports in the app while you are using the same device and browser. To ask us to remove something else, such as a photo that shows you, write to the contact above and tell us which report. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have rights over your personal data, including to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to ask for erasure or blocking, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

10. Security

The site uses HTTPS and the database is protected by access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what you are comfortable making public.

11. Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
