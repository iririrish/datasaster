1. What DATAsaster is

DATAsaster is a student-built demonstration. It shows a map, community incident reports, news, live data from outside services and official advisories posted by agency accounts.

2. It is not an emergency or official warning service

Do not rely on DATAsaster to keep you safe. In an emergency call 911 or your local emergency number. For official warnings use PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC, BFP and your local government. Reports from the public are unverified, can be wrong or out of date, and are not sent to any agency. Live data and news come from outside services and may be late, missing or inaccurate. An agency assessment is that agency's view and does not prove or disprove a report.

3. Posting reports

  • Post only what you have seen yourself or have the right to share. Be accurate.
  • Do not post false, misleading or spam reports, or reports to cause panic.
  • Do not post other people's private information, identity documents, faces of private people or content that is unlawful, abusive or harassing.
  • Do not impersonate an agency, an official or another person. This includes typing someone else's name, or an agency's name, in the optional name field.
  • Stay safe. Do not enter floodwater, fire, smoke or unstable ground to take a photo or find a location.

We may hide or delete content, or block access, if it breaks these terms or seems unsafe. Reports cannot be edited after posting. You can resolve or delete your own.

5. Agency and administrator accounts

Media accounts can add reports under their outlet name but cannot resolve or delete anything, and their reports remain subject to flagging like any other. Only an authorized representative may use an agency or media account, and each person has their own account. Agencies must publish only information they have issued, with a link to the original source, and must correct or withdraw mistakes promptly. Actions are logged. Access can be suspended or removed at any time. Taking part does not mean an agency endorses DATAsaster.
